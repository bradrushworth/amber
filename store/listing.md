    Amber Electric Dashboard

## Short description (80 char limit)

    Live prices, daily costs and solar feed-in for your Amber Electric account.

## Full description (4000 char limit)

See what your electricity is actually costing you, right now.

Amber Electric Dashboard turns your Amber account's live pricing and usage into
fast, readable charts: the current price, what today has cost so far, and a
scrollable history of every day and week.

WHAT YOU GET
• Right now — the live buy price, the current period (off-peak, shoulder, peak,
  solar sponge) and a warning when prices spike
• Today so far — a running dollar total, supply charge included
• Prices for yesterday, today and tomorrow — buy price and feed-in price as a
  matching pair for each day
• Days — every day's cost, usage or prices; tap a day for a half-hourly
  breakdown with totals for the day
• Weeks — the last four weeks at a glance
• Rotate to landscape and each pair of charts sits side by side
• Colour-coded by tariff period, with solar feed-in drawn below the line

SET UP ONCE
1. Sign in at app.amber.com.au and turn on "For Developers"
2. Generate an API token
3. Paste it into Settings

Your token and your data stay on your device. Nothing is sent to us — the app
talks only to Amber's own API.

GOOD TO KNOW
This app is only useful to you if you are an Amber Electric customer in
Australia. Accounts with more than one site are supported, and closed sites are
labelled so you can tell them apart.

Amber Electric Dashboard is not affiliated with Amber Electric other than that
we are a customer of their electricity services. The name Amber Electric is
their trademark.

## What's new (500 char limit)

A rebuilt interface: a live price panel up top, then buy and feed-in prices
paired for yesterday, today and tomorrow. New Days and Weeks tabs let you scroll
your history and tap any day for a half-hourly breakdown. Rotate to landscape
for side-by-side charts. Plus a new app icon, clearer chart labels and a
full-word legend.
